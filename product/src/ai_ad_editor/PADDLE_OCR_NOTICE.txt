Agent Video Kit local OCR adaptation

The DB detection preprocessing/postprocessing, perspective crop, normalization
and CTC decoding follow the public PaddleOCR and RapidOCR implementations.

Changes: explicit local model/Python/evidence registration and hashing,
CPU-only sequential ONNX execution, analytic minimum-rectangle expansion,
bounded frame/box/recognition budgets, exact supplied frame selection and PTS
validation, local report provenance, process-tree cleanup, no downloader,
provider, CLI wrapper or remote dictionary access.

Development model source: rapidocr-onnxruntime 1.3.25 PyPI wheel,
SHA256 3d8e5416f5bf4ab6566009dfdf24175ff80be937f633578c93befb1b526ff587.
The ch_PP-OCRv4_det_infer.onnx and ch_PP-OCRv4_rec_infer.onnx artifacts derive
from PaddleOCR. Model files remain external explicit local inputs and are not
included in this product distribution. Source/weight rights are recorded
separately in each project's Rights Ledger.

Primary source/license snapshots and model acquisition hashes are retained in
docs/validation/evidence/ocr-acquisition-20261019-v62/ in the source repository.
